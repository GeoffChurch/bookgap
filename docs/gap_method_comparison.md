# Gap-detection method comparison: 17/1702/T10181

Top-20 gaps from each pipeline, side by side. Three methods are compared:

- **K-ball** — the original size-ranked baseline (radius-based nearest-neighbor search around each book; basins are pure-article neighborhoods that exclude books).
- **Soft-EM (article-as-seed)** — the cohesion-aware production pipeline (moving-centroid HNSW, soft-membership posteriors, strict policy, min_post≥0.5 boundary, logit_sum ranking).
- **HDBSCAN max-bookless** — density-based; runs HDBSCAN's single-linkage tree over articles+books and extracts the *maximal bookless* cluster at each subtree (the largest pure-article cluster before any book joins). Size-ranked.

Keywords use log-odds-ratio (Fightin' Words) with marginal background and freq-weighting, with bigram phrase mining, matching the curriculum production pipeline. Top books are the most-voted nearest book per gap article (1-NN).

## Artifact recall summary

Counts of `templated-leak` basins (gaps whose articles are unified by *templated* lexical patterns — datasets, names, model brands — rather than a coherent research direction; flagged by the vote-concentration + title-overlap heuristic in `gap_book_existence_filter`). The headline is how many such basins each pipeline surfaces — more is *better* here, since they're artifacts the downstream curriculum-builder filters out, and surfacing them lets the heuristic catch more bad gaps before LLM proposal.

| pipeline | total basins | templated-leak | TL size range | basin size range |
|----------|--------------|----------------|---------------|------------------|
| K-ball | 200 | 8 | 33–98 | 33–387 |
| Soft-EM (article-seed) | 423 | 51 | 5–148 | 5–429 |
| HDBSCAN max-bookless | 280 | 173 | 1–98 | 1–98 |

**Templated-leak basin overlap (Jaccard ≥ 0.5):**

| pair | matched basins |
|------|-----------------|
| K-ball ↔ Soft-EM | 2 |
| K-ball ↔ HDBSCAN | 2 |
| Soft-EM ↔ HDBSCAN | 6 |

Low pairwise overlap indicates each method surfaces a mostly-disjoint set of templated leaks — they're complementary, not redundant. K-ball and Soft-EM tend to miss the smallest leak basins; HDBSCAN's max-bookless extraction picks them up because tiny dense clumps that haven't yet absorbed any book remain visible in the single-linkage tree.
